Memory Experiment

Phase 1: Study

You will see pairs of words. For some, you will simply read the full pair (e.g., FAST - SLOW). These will disappear on their own after a few seconds.

For others, you will see a word and a word fragment (e.g., FAST - S___). Type the completed word and press ENTER. Each underscore stands for one missing letter.

Pay attention to every pair. Your memory will be tested later.

Press SPACE to continue

Completing Word Fragments

The fragment always shows the first letter of the word you need to produce. Type the whole word, including that first letter.

FAST - S___

Correct: type SLOW and press ENTER.
Incorrect: typing only LOW (do not leave out the first letter).

Upper- or lower-case letters are both fine.

Press SPACE to begin Phase 1

Phase 2: Math

Complete as many math problems as you can in 2 minutes.

Type your answer and press ENTER to move to the next problem. The task will end automatically when time runs out.

Press SPACE to begin

Phase 3: Memory Test

You will now see a cue word. Type the TARGET word that was paired with or generated from this concept in Phase 1.

Some cue words are the exact words you saw in Phase 1; others are different words with a similar meaning. Press ENTER to submit. If you cannot remember, make your best guess or press ENTER to skip.

Press SPACE to begin
