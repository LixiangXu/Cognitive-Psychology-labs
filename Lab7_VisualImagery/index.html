Mental Imagery Experiment

In this experiment you will read short sentences that describe simple physical scenes: an object being thrown, something dropping into water, or a light casting a shadow. Your job is to form a clear mental image of each scene, then adjust a picture on the screen so that it matches your image.

There are no right or wrong answers for the imagery questions. We are interested in how people picture what they read. Some other parts measure how fast you read and how fast you can use the mouse.

The experiment takes about 20 minutes and runs in full screen. Clicking Start means you agree to take part.
