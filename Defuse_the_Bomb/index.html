Defuse the Bomb

A two-person communication game. The defuser sees the bomb; the expert sees only the manual. Neither can solve it alone: talk. Open #bomb on the defuser’s screen and #manual on the expert’s. Three strikes or 0:00 and it explodes.

Wires

  • 3 wires: any red? cut the first red. Else, last wire white? cut the first wire. Else cut the last wire.
  • 4 wires: more than one blue? cut the last blue. Else, serial ends in an odd digit and there is a red? cut the last red. Else, no yellow? cut the second wire. Else cut the first.
  • 5 wires: last wire black and serial ends odd? cut the fourth. Else, exactly one yellow and more than one blue? cut the first. Else, no black? cut the third. Else cut the first black.

Button

  1. Button is blue and says ABORT: press and release right away.
  2. More than 2 batteries and it says DETONATE: press and release right away.
  3. Button is white and the serial contains a vowel: press and release right away.
  4. Otherwise, press and hold. A strip lights up: blue strip → release when the timer’s seconds contain a 4; yellow → contain a 5; any other color → contain a 1.

Keypad

All four symbols on the bomb appear in one column. Press them in that column’s top-to-bottom order.
